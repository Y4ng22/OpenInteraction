# OpenInteraction Guide

## What is OpenInteraction?

OpenInteraction is a multimodal system for natural realtime interaction. It continuously processes camera and microphone input while producing speech and text responses.

## How do I start a live conversation?

Allow camera and microphone access, then select **Start**. Once the status is online, speak naturally; you may also interrupt the assistant while it is responding.

## Why is the camera preview black?

Open the physical privacy shutter, disable the operating system's camera privacy mode, and close other applications using the camera. Then stop the session, hard-refresh the page, and start again.

## What can the OpenInteraction assistant see?

It processes only the camera frames and microphone audio sent by your browser during the current session. Do not expose passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive material.

## What does Force Listen do?

Force Listen interrupts the current response and prioritizes your new input. Headphones reduce speaker echo and improve interruption behavior.

## Can I change the response style?

Yes. Presets control language and speaking style, while Advanced settings expose the system prompt and reference voice. The OpenInteraction identity remains attached automatically.

## When should I enable HD Vision?

Use HD Vision for text, screens, and fine visual details. It sends more visual information and may add a small amount of latency.

## How do I use fullscreen mode?

Select the fullscreen icon in the video panel. Start, pause, stop, and subtitle controls remain available there.
